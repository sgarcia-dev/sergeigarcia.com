I started my career at Accenture in Monterrey, Mexico, building front end and mobile apps for clients in agriculture and healthcare. I joined as a junior developer and soon took on work beyond that scope, from performance to testing and team practices.

### What I did

- Built a hybrid mobile app for an agriculture client with Ionic, Cordova, AngularJS, Couchbase and ArcGIS. It brought farm-management workflows and precision-agriculture information into one app to support better planning and decisions.
- Improved rendering performance, with a reported 3 to 5 times speedup.
- Added tests, development tooling and development-process improvements.
- Built a responsive website for a U.S. healthcare client.
- Delivered an iOS hospital demo with C# and Xamarin Forms within four weeks.

### Mentoring and teamwork

- Wrote coding standards and onboarding guidance to help other developers get productive.
