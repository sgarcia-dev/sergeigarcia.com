DigitalOnUs was a software consulting company in Monterrey, Mexico. As a Senior Software Engineer there, I worked for Intelius and led full-stack development of its checkout flow, from the Vue front end down to the GraphQL layer.

### What I did

- <span class="lv-mark">Led full-stack development of the Intelius checkout flow</span> with Vue, Vuex, Nuxt, Apollo, GraphQL, Express and Node.js.
- Wrote GraphQL resolvers that simplified complex data requests and made the code easier to work with.
- Brought a legacy Ember application up to more maintainable standards while keeping its browser support.

### Mentoring and teamwork

- Started regular Vue and GraphQL workshops when the team needed more confidence with the stack, with practical examples instead of leaving people to learn it alone.
- <span class="lv-mark">Coached two junior engineers</span> through monthly conversations about their goals and career growth.

DigitalOnUs was later acquired by Tech Mahindra.
